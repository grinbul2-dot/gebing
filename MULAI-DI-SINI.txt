WORD CRYSTAL QUEST - THINGS AROUND ME

START PLAYING
1. Unzip the whole folder.
2. Open index.html in a modern browser.
3. Choose Start adventure. Type your name and choose a picture.
4. Keep all files and the assets folder together.
5. On a laptop, you can play offline. On phones, use the web game if your
   device cannot open local HTML files. Turn the screen sideways.

CONTROLS
1 / 2 / 3: set aim or lane; cut a fruit box; answer a test question. P / Esc: pause. M: sound on or off.
School: A/D move; W / Space / Z jump; S duck.
Home: WASD aim; Space / X / F shoot.
Town: A/D change lanes; W / Shift run faster; S slow down.
Finale: A/D choose; Space / X / F cut. You can swipe or click too.

LIVES AND TIME
Start an area with 3 lives. Wrong answers, missed moves and time-outs cost
1 life. Safe jumps and landings on clear ground do not cost a life.
At 0 lives, it is GAME OVER. Try the area again from mission 1.
Normal mode: 60 seconds per mission, 45 seconds per test question.
Easy mode: 90 seconds per mission, 60 seconds per test question.
The clock stops during pauses and messages or when the game window is hidden.
A test time-out counts as a wrong answer. Tests do not use game lives.

A FRESH START
Reset Progress is on the map. Confirm to clear only the current player's
scores, tests, badges, stars, word cards, styles and saved missions. Their name and picture stay.
Other players keep their progress. This cannot be undone.

TEST ALL MAPS
Choose SECRET CODE. Type SURYO AGUNG and press Enter.
All maps open. Scores and badges must still be earned.
To turn this off, choose TEST MODE ON > Turn off test mode.
Reset Progress also turns test mode off.

YOUR FILES

DOKUMENTASI-DESAIN.md: design notes, sources and asset records.
FONT-LICENSE.txt: the font licence.

YOUR RESULTS
Results stay on this browser/device. Teacher results > Download CSV saves
another copy. Clearing browser data may remove your progress.
Get 70 or more in all four tests to download a certificate PNG.

YOUR NEW ADVENTURE
Your move is your answer: jump onto a platform, shoot a word target,
run into a gate, or cut a fruit box. Choosing an aim does not give an answer.
After 4 missions, lights come on. After 8, plants grow. After 12, the world
is full of life. Finish the 3-step Final Challenge to open the area test.
My Word Book keeps word cards and short examples. Practise wrong answers
without a clock or lives. Two correct practice answers clear a word.
Each area has 3 stars: finish the missions; answer 12 different questions correctly
on your first try, without hints; finish the Final Challenge.
Open Stars & Style to wear rewards at 3, 6, 9 and 12 stars. Styles change
only your look. They never change lives, time or test scores.
Old players keep their tests, badges and certificates. They can play the
new challenges to earn new stars.

PLAY FEEDBACK
The map shows your next world and finished missions. A bright outline shows
your chosen aim or lane. It does not show the correct answer. Watch for
Great move and combo messages. Turn on Less background movement in Settings
if you want fewer effects. Sound can be turned off at any time.

MORE QUESTIONS, NEW CUT MOVES
Each map has 50 questions. A new run picks 12: four at each level.
The next new run uses different questions. Answers move to new positions.
Continue a saved run to keep the same questions and answer order.
In the fourth map, your explorer flies with a sword and cuts a fruit box.
The answer is checked when the sword hits. One wrong cut costs one life.
